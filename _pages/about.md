I am a Master’s student in **Data Science & Artificial Intelligence**, currently a Research Intern at **EDF R&D**. My work focuses on **LLM Interpretability**, specifically using **Sparse Autoencoders (SAE)** to disentangle dense embeddings into monosemantic features.

### Research Interests
My primary goal is to open the "black box" of Large Language Models. I am fascinated by:
* **Mechanistic Interpretability:** Understanding how internal representations map to human-understandable concepts.
* **Sparse Coding:** Using SAEs to overcome the superposition hypothesis.
* **Generative Modeling:** From Neural SDEs to multimodal RAG systems.

### Current Work
At EDF R&D (Sequoia Department), I am working on explaining text embeddings. By training Sparse Autoencoders, we aim to isolate specific features (concepts) within the latent space to improve model transparency and reliability in industrial applications.

### Background
Before focusing on research, I developed several MVPs in the industry:
* **At Paris Digital Lab**, I built multimodal RAG systems (ColPali) for Thales and integrated LLMs with complex APIs (Anthropic MCP) for SNCF.
* **At Emotors**, I designed thermal estimators for electric motors using machine learning.
